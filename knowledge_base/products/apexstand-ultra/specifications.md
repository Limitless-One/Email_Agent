# ApexStand Ultra Ergonomic Aluminum Laptop Stand - Specifications

## Overview
The **ApexStand Ultra** is a premium desktop laptop riser engineered from CNC-machined aerospace-grade aluminum alloy. Featuring a precision dual-axis hinge system, it allows continuous height and tilt angle adjustments to elevate screens up to eye level, reducing cervical spine strain and promoting ergonomic posture.
