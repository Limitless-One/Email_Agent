# ApexStand Ultra Laptop Stand - Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Will this stand fit my 16-inch MacBook Pro or 17-inch Dell XPS?
**A:** Yes! The ApexStand Ultra is engineered to accommodate laptops ranging from **10.0 inches up to 17.3 inches**. Its cradle plate measures 10.82" x 9.45", and the front retaining hooks (0.63" / 16mm height) securely hold thick chassis laptops weighing up to **13.2 lbs (6.0 kg)**.

### Q2: Can I use this stand with an iPad Pro, drawing tablet, or portable monitor?
**A:** Yes. The stand works exceptionally well for iPads (10.9", 11", 12.9"), graphics tablets, and secondary USB-C portable monitors. The dual-hinge design allows you to adjust it to a low 15°–25° drafting angle ideal for stylus drawing and note-taking.

### Q3: Will the aluminum edges scratch my laptop or desk?
**A:** No. Every contact point is protected:
- The base features 4 dense non-marking silicone pads that protect wood, glass, and laminate surfaces.
- The top cradle features two wide silicone back strips.
- The two front retaining hooks are fully encased in protective rubberized silicone, preventing any direct metal-on-metal contact.

### Q4: Does the stand fold completely flat for travel?
**A:** Yes. When fully collapsed, the stand measures **10.82" x 9.45" x 1.97" (5.0 cm thick)** and weighs 2.38 lbs (1.08 kg). It slides easily into most laptop bags, backpacks, and carry-on luggage.

### Q5: Does the stand help with laptop cooling?
**A:** Yes, significantly. Unlike sitting flat on a desk where heat gets trapped underneath the chassis, the ApexStand Ultra features dual teardrop thermal cutouts and raises the laptop into open air, facilitating passive convection cooling and lowering CPU/GPU temperatures by up to 5°C to 8°C during heavy tasks.

### Q6: Can I type directly on my laptop while it is elevated on the stand?
**A:** While the stand is rigid enough to support light typing, ergonomic best practices dictate that any laptop elevated higher than 3 to 4 inches should be paired with an **external keyboard and mouse**. Typing on a raised, angled laptop keyboard causes wrist extension and shoulder tension.

### Q7: What should I do if my stand feels too loose or too stiff to adjust?
**A:** Use the included 4mm Allen/hex wrench to adjust the tension screws located inside the hinge joints. Turn clockwise to tighten (for heavier laptops) or counter-clockwise to loosen (for easier repositioning).
