# ApexStand Ultra Laptop Stand - User Manual & Operating Guide

## 1. Initial Setup & Unfolding
1. Remove the stand from the protective bubble sleeve and place the heavy base flat on a stable, level desk surface.
2. Hold down the base plate firmly with one hand.
3. With your other hand, pull upward on the main support arm until it reaches your desired elevation.
4. Tilt the top cradle platform until it faces you at a comfortable reading angle (typically 15° to 30° from horizontal).
5. Ensure the two front retaining hooks face upward before placing your laptop onto the cradle.

> **Important Safety Precaution**: Always place the stand on a flat, non-slick surface. Ensure both retaining hooks are completely unobstructed so the laptop cannot slide off.

---

## 2. Adjusting Hinge Tension
The ApexStand Ultra comes pre-tightened from the factory with high-torque friction dampers to hold up to 13.2 lbs (6 kg) without sagging. Over time or depending on personal preference, you may wish to adjust the resistance:

1. Locate the round hinge cap on the side of each joint (one on the base hinge, one on the upper cradle hinge).
2. Insert the included **4mm Allen/Hex key** into the hexagonal screw socket inside the hinge pivot.
3. **To tighten the hinge (if holding a heavier 16"–17" workstation/gaming laptop)**:
   - Turn the hex key **clockwise** in small quarter-turn (90°) increments.
   - Test stability by gently applying downward pressure.
4. **To loosen the hinge (if the stand is difficult to fold/unfold)**:
   - Turn the hex key **counter-clockwise** in small quarter-turn increments.
   - Do not overtighten or unscrew completely.

---

## 3. Recommended Ergonomic Setup Guide
To maximize neck, shoulder, and back comfort while using the ApexStand Ultra:
- **Screen Eye Level**: Adjust the top of your laptop's screen so that it sits at or slightly below eye level (approx. 2 to 3 inches below eye height).
- **Viewing Distance**: Position the laptop about an arm’s length away (20 to 28 inches / 50 to 70 cm).
- **External Keyboard & Mouse Recommendation**: When using the stand at full elevation (higher than 3 inches), we strongly recommend using an external keyboard and mouse on the desk. Direct typing on an elevated laptop keyboard puts strain on the wrists and shoulders.
- **Cable Management**: Route power and display cables beneath the cradle and through the open cutout between the support arms to maintain a clutter-free desk.

---

## 4. Folding for Storage & Travel
1. Remove the laptop from the cradle.
2. Press down firmly on the upper cradle until it folds parallel against the support arm.
3. Push the support arm downward until it nests completely flush into the recessed groove of the base plate.
4. Slide the stand into a laptop sleeve or backpack compartment (requires minimum 11" x 10" compartment space).

---

## 5. Cleaning & Maintenance
- **Aluminum Surface**: Wipe clean with a dry microfiber cloth or a cloth lightly dampened with water or 70% isopropyl alcohol. Avoid abrasive cleaning powders, acidic cleaners, or steel wool that could damage the anodized coating.
- **Silicone Pads**: Over time, dust may reduce the grip of the silicone pads. Wipe the pads with a lint-free damp cloth to restore tackiness.
- **Spare Parts**: If any silicone feet peel off or get lost, use the two replacement adhesive pads provided in the original packaging.
