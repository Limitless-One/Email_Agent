# ApexStand Ultra Laptop Stand - Troubleshooting Guide

This guide provides step-by-step diagnostic and resolution procedures for common customer inquiries regarding the ApexStand Ultra.

---

### Issue 1: The stand slowly sags or sinks downward when a heavy laptop is placed on it
* **Root Cause**: The internal friction washers in the dual hinges need additional torque to counter the weight of larger laptops (especially 15.6"–17.3" gaming or workstation laptops weighing over 5 lbs).
* **Diagnostic Step**: Check if the sagging occurs at the lower hinge (base) or upper hinge (cradle).
* **Resolution**:
  1. Remove the laptop from the stand.
  2. Take the **4mm Allen/Hex key** included in the box (or any standard metric 4mm hex wrench).
  3. Insert the key into the socket screw on the side of the sagging hinge.
  4. Turn the screw **clockwise 90° to 180°** to increase clamping pressure.
  5. Place the laptop back on the stand to verify stability. Repeat if minor sinking persists.
* **Escalation Path**: If the screw spins freely without tightening, or if the internal thread is stripped, the unit qualifies for immediate warranty replacement under manufacturing defect guidelines.

---

### Issue 2: The hinges are extremely stiff and hard to unfold out of the box
* **Root Cause**: ApexStand Ultra hinges are engineered with high initial factory tension so that they remain firm across years of daily use. They are intentionally stiff when brand new.
* **Resolution**:
  1. This is normal behavior and not a defect.
  2. Place the stand on a sturdy desk. Place one hand firmly flat on the base plate to anchor it, then pull firmly on the upper arm with your dominant hand.
  3. If adjustment requires uncomfortable force, insert the 4mm hex key into both hinge screws and rotate **counter-clockwise by one quarter turn (45° to 90°)**.
  4. *Warning*: Do not loosen by more than 180° at one time, as this may cause the cradle to collapse under a laptop.

---

### Issue 3: The stand wobbles or rocks on the desk
* **Root Cause**: Desk surface irregularity, debris under the silicone pads, or an unevenly distributed hinge angle.
* **Diagnostic Steps**:
  1. Check desk flatness: Move the stand to a known flat surface (such as a granite countertop or glass table).
  2. Check bottom feet: Verify that all four silicone feet are intact and clean on the bottom of the base plate.
* **Resolution**:
  - If a silicone pad is missing, apply one of the 2 spare self-adhesive silicone pads included in the box.
  - If the base plate got slightly warped during shipping transit, gently press downward on the diagonally opposing corners while holding the base against a flat table.
  - If the wobble is due to direct heavy typing on the laptop keyboard while elevated, remind the customer that elevating laptops higher than 4 inches is ergonomically designed for use with an external keyboard and mouse.

---

### Issue 4: The front retaining hooks interfere with typing or press against wrists
* **Root Cause**: Retaining hooks are 16mm (0.63 in) tall to prevent thick gaming laptops from falling off.
* **Resolution**:
  - When the laptop is elevated, the user should be utilizing an external peripheral keyboard.
  - If using the stand at its lowest flat tilt (approx. 10°–15°) for direct typing, slide the laptop backwards slightly so that the front bevel rests gently against the silicone lining rather than overhangs.

---

### Issue 5: Customer lost the included Allen/Hex key
* **Resolution**:
  - The hex socket is a universally standard **metric 4mm hex key (Allen wrench)** available at any hardware or home improvement store.
  - Alternatively, if the customer registered their warranty, NovaGear customer service will ship a complimentary replacement accessory kit (SKU: `NG-ACC-HK4`) free of charge.
