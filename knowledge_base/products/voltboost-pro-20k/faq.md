# VoltBoost Pro 20K Power Bank - Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Can I take the VoltBoost Pro 20K on commercial flights?
**A:** Yes! The VoltBoost Pro 20K has a capacity rating of **74 Watt-hours (Wh)** (20,000mAh at 3.7V). FAA, TSA, and international aviation regulations permit lithium-ion batteries up to **100Wh** in **carry-on baggage** without airline pre-approval. 
> ⚠️ **Important:** You must pack it in your carry-on or personal item. Airline safety regulations strictly forbid spare lithium power banks in checked baggage.

### Q2: Can this power bank charge my laptop while I am actively working on it?
**A:** Yes. Port USB-C1 supplies up to 65W Power Delivery, which is sufficient to actively power and charge 13", 14", and many 15" laptops (including MacBook Air, MacBook Pro 13"/14", Dell XPS 13, Lenovo ThinkPad X1, HP Spectre x360, and ASUS Zenbook) even under active workload.

### Q3: How many times can it charge my phone?
**A:** On average:
- **iPhone 15 / 16 / 14 / 13**: ~4.2 full charges
- **Samsung Galaxy S24 / S23**: ~3.8 full charges
- **iPad Air / Pro 11"**: ~1.8 full charges
- **MacBook Air 13" (M2/M3)**: ~1.2 full charges
- **Nintendo Switch (OLED)**: ~2.8 full charges

### Q4: Does the power bank get warm while charging? Is that dangerous?
**A:** Delivering or receiving 65 Watts of power generates normal thermal dissipation. The aluminum perimeter acts as a heat sink to draw heat away from the internal battery cells. Temperatures up to 45°C (113°F) are completely normal. The device contains dual NTC thermal safety sensors that will automatically shut off power if temperatures ever exceed 65°C (`E01` warning).

### Q5: Can I charge 3 devices at the same time?
**A:** Yes. When all 3 ports (USB-C1, USB-C2, USB-A) are utilized simultaneously, intelligent power splitting delivers:
- USB-C1: 45W (ideal for laptop or tablet)
- USB-C2 + USB-A: 15W combined shared (ideal for two phones or accessories)
- Total combined power: 60W

### Q6: Does it support Samsung "Super Fast Charging 2.0" (45W)?
**A:** Yes. Port USB-C1 supports Programmable Power Supply (PPS) protocol up to 11V/4A (44W-45W), enabling full-speed Samsung Super Fast Charging 2.0 on Galaxy S24 Ultra, S23 Ultra, and Galaxy Tab S9 series.

### Q7: Why does the power bank stop charging my Apple Watch or wireless earbuds?
**A:** Low-power accessories draw less than 50mA of current, causing standard power banks to shut down to conserve energy. Simply **double-click the power button** to enter **Low-Current / Trickle Charging Mode** (indicated by an animated `[o]` on the OLED display).
