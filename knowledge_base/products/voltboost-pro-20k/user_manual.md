# VoltBoost Pro 20K Power Bank - User Manual & Operating Guide

## 1. Quick Start & Button Functions
The power button is located on the right side of the device:
* **Single Press (1x Click)**: Wakes the OLED display screen, showing the current battery percentage (%) and remaining standby status.
* **Double Press (2x Quick Click)**: Activates **Low-Current / Trickle Charging Mode** for sensitive, low-power devices (e.g., Apple AirPods, Galaxy Buds, smartwatches, fitness trackers, wireless mice). When active, an animated rotating icon `[o]` appears on the screen.
* **Exit Low-Current Mode**: Single press the button again to return to standard high-output charging. (The mode will also automatically shut off after 2 hours).
* **Press & Hold (5 Seconds)**: Manually powers off the output ports and display screen.

---

## 2. Reading the Smart OLED Display Screen
The high-contrast OLED screen provides real-time diagnostic information:

1. **Percentage Indicator (`0%` - `100%`)**: Current stored battery reserve.
2. **Wattage Indicator (`W`)**: Real-time wattage being drawn from or delivered to the power bank.
   - Example: When charging a 60W laptop, the display reads `58.4W OUT`.
   - Example: When recharging the power bank with a 65W wall adapter, it reads `62.1W IN`.
3. **Green Lightning Bolt Icon (`⚡`)**: Appears when high-voltage Fast Charging (Power Delivery 3.0, PPS, or QC 3.0) is actively negotiated.
4. **Error Notification Codes**:
   - `E01`: Over-temperature detected (unit will halt charging until cooled down).
   - `E02`: Short-circuit or over-current protection tripped on a connected cable/device.
   - `E03`: Low battery voltage threshold reached (recharge required).

## 3. How to Fast-Charge Your Devices
### For Laptops (MacBook, Dell XPS, ThinkPad, Surface, Chromebook):
- **Always plug into USB-C1**. USB-C1 is the dedicated 65W high-output port.
- Use a **100W or 60W rated USB-C to USB-C cable** (such as the included cable or NovaGear NexusCord). Using an unrated or legacy cable will limit output to 18W–30W.

### For Smartphones & Tablets:
- **iPhones (iPhone 8 through iPhone 16)**: Connect to USB-C1 or USB-C2 using a USB-C to Lightning or USB-C to USB-C cable. Will fast-charge at 20W–27W.
- **Samsung Galaxy (S20 through S24 Ultra)**: Connect to USB-C1 for Samsung Super Fast Charging (PPS 25W/45W).
- **USB-A Devices**: Connect to the USB-A port for Quick Charge 3.0 compatible Android devices (up to 22.5W).

---

## 4. How to Recharge the VoltBoost Pro 20K
1. Connect a USB-C cable to the **USB-C1 port** (the only input port).
2. Connect the other end to a high-output USB-C wall charger (not included):
   - **Recommended**: 65W USB-C PD Wall Charger (Full charge in ~1.5 hours).
   - **Compatible**: 30W USB-C Charger (~2.8 hours) or 20W Charger (~4.5 hours).
   - **Not recommended**: Standard 5W phone charging blocks (will take over 16 hours to recharge).
3. The display will light up with the charging animation and show `[XX] W IN`.

---

## 5. Pass-Through Charging Feature
The VoltBoost Pro 20K supports simultaneous charging and discharging:
- You can plug a 65W wall charger into **USB-C1 (In)** and connect your phone or accessories to **USB-C2 (Out)** or **USB-A (Out)**.
- *Note*: During pass-through operation, maximum output to secondary devices is regulated at 15W to protect internal battery chemistry and avoid thermal spikes.

---

## 6. Battery Longevity Best Practices
- **Storage**: If storing the power bank for prolonged periods without use, keep the charge level between **50% and 70%** in a cool, dry place (avoid leaving inside parked cars in summer).
- **Cycle Count**: High-grade Li-Po cells retain >80% original capacity after 500+ full discharge/charge cycles.
- **Flight Travel**: Always pack the power bank in your **carry-on baggage**. Aviation rules strictly prohibit external lithium batteries in checked baggage.
