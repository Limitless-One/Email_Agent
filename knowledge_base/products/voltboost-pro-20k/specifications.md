# VoltBoost Pro 20,000mAh 65W Power Bank - Specifications

## Overview
The **VoltBoost Pro 20K** is a high-capacity, airline-compliant portable battery pack engineered to fast-charge USB-C laptops, tablets, smartphones, and low-draw peripherals. Utilizing high-density Lithium-Polymer cells and intelligent GaN power management, it delivers up to **65W Power Delivery (PD 3.0)** from a single USB-C port, capable of charging a 13" MacBook Pro from 0% to 50% in approximately 45 minutes.

## Port Configurations & Power Allocation

### Single Port Operation:
* **USB-C1 (Bi-directional Input / Output)**:
  * *Input*: 5V/3A, 9V/3A, 12V/3A, 15V/3A, 20V/3.25A (65W Max)
  * *Output*: 5V/3A, 9V/3A, 12V/3A, 15V/3A, 20V/3.25A (65W Max PD 3.0 / PPS: 3.3V-11V/4A)
* **USB-C2 (Output Only)**:
  * 5V/3A, 9V/3A, 12V/2.5A, 15V/2A (30W Max PD 3.0)
* **USB-A (Output Only)**:
  * 4.5V/5A, 5V/4.5A, 5V/3A, 9V/2A, 12V/1.5A (22.5W Max QC 3.0 / SCP / FCP)

### Multi-Port Simultaneous Distribution:
* **USB-C1 + USB-C2**: 45W (C1) + 20W (C2) = **65W Total**
* **USB-C1 + USB-A**: 45W (C1) + 18W (USB-A) = **63W Total**
* **USB-C2 + USB-A**: Shared 15W Max (5V/3A split)
* **USB-C1 + USB-C2 + USB-A**: 45W (C1) + 15W shared across (C2 + USB-A) = **60W Total**

## Safety & Protection Systems
- Dual NTC Temperature Sensors (Over-temperature protection cutoff at 65°C / 149°F)
- Over-charge and Over-discharge protection circuit
- Output Short-Circuit Protection with auto-recovery
- Over-voltage and Over-current protection
- Pass-Through Charging Support (Charge the power bank while simultaneously charging a connected device via USB-C2 or USB-A)
