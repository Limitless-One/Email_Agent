# NexusCord 100W Braided Cable - Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: What is an "E-Marker" chip and why does this cable need one?
**A:** Under USB-IF (USB Implementers Forum) standards, any Type-C cable that carries more than 3 Amperes of current (or greater than 60 Watts) **must** contain an Electronic ID Marker (E-Marker) microchip inside the connector shell. The E-Marker acts as a safety communicator between your charger and your laptop/phone, certifying that the cable has the 5A rating and heat shielding necessary to safely transmit up to 100 Watts without overheating.

### Q2: Does this cable work with the iPhone 15 and iPhone 16 series?
**A:** Yes, fully compatible. It works with iPhone 15, 15 Plus, 15 Pro, 15 Pro Max, and all iPhone 16 models. It will charge your iPhone at its maximum supported charging speed (reaching 50% in approximately 30 minutes when paired with a 20W, 30W, or higher USB-C wall charger).

### Q3: Does this cable support Samsung "Super Fast Charging 2.0" (45W)?
**A:** Yes! Samsung Galaxy S20/S21/S22/S23/S24 Ultra requires a 5-Ampere rated cable with PPS support to activate 45W Super Fast Charging 2.0. Because the NexusCord is a certified 100W / 5A cable, your Samsung phone will show the "Super Fast Charging 2.0" blue ring animation when plugged into a 45W+ PPS charger.

### Q4: Can I use this cable to connect my laptop to a portable or 4K monitor?
**A:** No. The NexusCord 100W is optimized for high-speed charging and standard data syncing (USB 2.0 / 480Mbps). It does not have the video lanes required for DisplayPort Alternate Mode, HDMI conversion, or 4K/60Hz video streaming. For monitor connections, you need a specialized USB4, Thunderbolt 3/4, or DisplayPort-over-USB-C cable.

### Q5: Can I use this cable in my car for Apple CarPlay or Android Auto?
**A:** Yes, provided your car has a native USB-C port that supports wired data communication. The cable handles USB 2.0 480Mbps data, which is more than sufficient for CarPlay and Android Auto music streaming and GPS mapping.

### Q6: Can I use a 100W cable with a 20W or 30W charger? Will it damage my device?
**A:** It is 100% safe. The 100W rating is a *maximum capacity rating*, not a force-feed. The device being charged communicates with the charger and only draws the exact amperage and voltage it needs. Using a 100W cable with a smaller charger or smaller phone causes zero risk of damage.
