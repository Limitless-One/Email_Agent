# NexusCord 100W Braided Cable - User Manual & Operating Guide

## 1. Compatibility & General Use
The NexusCord 100W is a universal charging and data cable for any hardware device that utilizes a standard USB-C port:
- **Laptops**: MacBook Pro (13", 14", 15", 16"), MacBook Air, Dell XPS (13/15/17), Lenovo ThinkPad/Yoga, HP Spectre/Envy, ASUS ZenBook/ROG, Microsoft Surface Pro/Laptop, Acer Swift.
- **Tablets**: iPad Pro (all USB-C models), iPad Air (4th gen and later), iPad mini (6th gen and later), Samsung Galaxy Tab S8/S9.
- **Smartphones**: iPhone 15/16 series, Samsung Galaxy S20 through S24 Ultra, Google Pixel 6/7/8/9 series, OnePlus, Moto.
- **Gaming & Accessories**: Nintendo Switch, Steam Deck, ASUS ROG Ally, NovaGear VoltBoost Pro 20K, wireless headphones, drones.

---

## 2. Unlocking 100W Fast Charging
Charging speed depends on three components working in synergy:
1. **The Cable**: The NexusCord is rated for up to 100W (20V/5A) thanks to its integrated E-Marker chipset.
2. **The Charger (Power Source)**: Your wall brick or power bank must support high wattage. For example, plugging this cable into a 20W phone block will only supply 20W. To charge a 16" MacBook Pro at maximum speed, pair this cable with a 96W, 100W, or 140W USB-C PD power adapter.
3. **The Receiving Device**: The connected laptop or phone will only draw as much power as its internal power management allows (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro maxes out at ~27W; MacBook Pro 14" draws ~67W–96W).

---

## 3. Data Transfer Mode
- When connected between a computer and a smartphone, external SSD, or camera, the cable transfers data at **USB 2.0 speeds (up to 480 Mbps)**.
- It is ideal for syncing music, transferring photos, backing up devices, and moving documents.
- *Notice*: This cable does **not** support video transmission (DisplayPort Alternate Mode) or Thunderbolt speeds (40Gbps). See [Troubleshooting](file:///Users/harsha/Desktop/Projects/LabelOCR/knowledge_base/products/nexuscord-c-to-c/troubleshooting.md) for monitor connection questions.

## 4. Proper Storage & Handling Tips
- **Use the Cable Tie**: Always use the included silicone cable strap when coiling the cable for storage.
- **Avoid Pinching**: Avoid sharply bending the cable at the neck joint (strain relief boot) at angles tighter than 90°.
- **Disconnect Properly**: When unplugging, grasp the metal zinc-alloy housing rather than pulling on the braided fabric cord.
- **Moisture & Cleaning**: Ensure both USB-C male ends are completely dry before plugging into active power. Clean outer braiding using a damp cloth. Never submerge the connector heads in water or cleaning solutions.
