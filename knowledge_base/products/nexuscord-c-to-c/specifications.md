# NexusCord 100W Braided USB-C to USB-C Cable (2m) - Specifications

## Overview
The **NexusCord 100W** is a heavy-duty, double-braided nylon charging and syncing cable designed for demanding USB-C power requirements. Featuring an integrated **E-Marker (Electronic ID Marker) smart chip**, it negotiates up to 100W (20V/5A) Power Delivery safely, protecting device batteries from overvoltage and excessive heat.
