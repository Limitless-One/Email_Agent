# NovaGear Order Cancellation & Modification Policy

To ensure industry-leading delivery speeds, NovaGear utilizes an automated robotics fulfillment system. Once an order is placed, it is transmitted directly to our warehouse floor for picking, packing, and sorting.

---

## 1. The 30-Minute Self-Service Cancellation Window
- Customers can cancel their order or edit shipping information **within 30 minutes of order placement**:
  - Open the **Order Confirmation Email**.
  - Click the **"Edit or Cancel Order"** link at the bottom of the receipt.
  - Choose "Cancel Entire Order" or "Edit Shipping Address".
  - If cancelled, authorization holds on your credit card or PayPal account are voided immediately.

---

## 2. Cancellations After 30 Minutes
- **After 30 minutes**, orders enter automated fulfillment and queue for pallet loading.
- While our human support team will attempt an emergency warehouse stop if you email **support@novagear.com** with the subject line *"URGENT: Cancel Order #[Order Number]"*, **cancellation cannot be guaranteed** once picking has initiated.
- **Alternative Solution**: If the order cannot be intercepted in time, the customer can simply refuse delivery when the courier arrives, or use our [30-Day Hassle-Free Returns Portal](file:///Users/harsha/Desktop/Projects/LabelOCR/knowledge_base/policies/return_and_refund_policy.md) as soon as the package is delivered.

---

## 3. Modifying Items (Color, SKU, Quantity)
- Because our billing systems are tokenized for payment security, our agents **cannot add items or increase charges** to an existing order.
- If you wish to change a color (e.g., switch ApexStand Ultra from Silver to Space Gray) or add another NexusCord cable:
  - If within 30 minutes: Cancel the order via your confirmation link and place a new order.
  - If past 30 minutes: Contact support. We will place a complimentary return note on the original item so you can swap it at zero cost once delivered.

---

## 4. Address Corrections
- If you notice a typo in your street address, apartment number, or zip code:
  - Contact **support@novagear.com** or call **+1 (800) 555-0199** immediately.
  - If the package has already left the facility, we can initiate a **UPS Delivery Intercept** or **FedEx Package Reroute** (carrier processing fee of $15 may apply if carrier assesses a surcharge).
