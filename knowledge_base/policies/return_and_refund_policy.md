# NovaGear Return and Refund Policy

At NovaGear, we stand behind the design and quality of our gear. We offer a **30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee** on all direct purchases made via `novagear.com`.

---

## 1. The 30-Day Return Window
- Customers may return any eligible product within **30 calendar days from the date of confirmed carrier delivery**.
- Requests initiated after the 30-day window are not eligible for standard refund, but hardware issues remain protected under our [Warranty Policy](file:///Users/harsha/Desktop/Projects/LabelOCR/knowledge_base/policies/warranty_policy.md).

---

## 2. Eligibility & Condition Requirements
To qualify for a full refund:
- **Condition**: Items must be returned in good condition, free from user-induced physical damage, deep gouges, cracks, or unauthorized disassembly.
- **Original Contents**: Returns must include all original packaging, inner trays, user manuals, and included accessories (e.g., hex keys, cable straps, short charging cables).
- **Missing Items or Damaged Packaging**: Returns missing accessories or original retail packaging may be subject to a **15% repackaging fee**, deducted from the total refund.

---

| Reason for Return | Who Pays Shipping? | Label Type | Notes |

| **Defective / Damaged on Arrival** | **NovaGear (100% Free)** | Prepaid UPS/USPS Label | Must report within 7 days of delivery. Photos requested. |

## 4. Step-by-Step Return Process

```
[Customer Requests Return] -> [Support/Portal Issues RMA & Label] -> [Customer Drops at Carrier]
                                                                             |
[Refund Issued to Bank: 3-5 Days] <-- [Warehouse Inspects Unit: 2-3 Days] <--+
```

1. **Initiate Return**:
   - Visit the self-service returns portal at `https://returns.novagear.com` using your **Order Number** (e.g., `NG-104829`) and **billing zip code / email address**.
   - Or, email **support@novagear.com** with the subject line *"Return Request - Order #[Order Number]"*.
2. **Receive RMA & Return Label**:
   - Once approved, an automated Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) slip and prepaid digital shipping label will be sent via email.
3. **Pack the Item**:
   - Securely pack the item in its original box, insert the RMA slip, and affix the prepaid label over any previous shipping labels.
4. **Drop Off**:
   - Drop the package at any authorized UPS Store or USPS post office within **14 days** of receiving the label.

---

## 5. Refund Processing Timeline
- **Warehouse Inspection**: Once received at our fulfillment center, items undergo inspection within **2 to 3 business days**.
- **Refund Issuance**: Once approved, refunds are credited back to the original method of payment (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, PayPal, Apple Pay).
- **Bank Clearing Time**: 
  - Credit Cards: **3 to 5 business days** depending on the issuing bank.
  - PayPal / Apple Pay: **24 to 48 hours**.
- If a customer used a NovaGear Gift Card, funds are immediately restored to the digital gift card code.

---

## 6. Items Damaged in Transit (DOA)
If a package arrives visibly crushed, torn, or with broken contents:
1. Notify customer support within **7 days of delivery**.
2. Provide photos of the damaged shipping box and the damaged product.
3. NovaGear will immediately dispatch a brand-new replacement unit with **complimentary 2-day expedited shipping**, without waiting for the damaged item to return.
