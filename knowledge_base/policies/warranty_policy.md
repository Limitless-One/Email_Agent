# NovaGear Limited Hardware Warranty Policy

NovaGear provides a robust warranty program to protect our customers against material and manufacturing defects.

---

## 1. Warranty Periods

| Product | Standard Warranty | With Free Online Registration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ApexStand Ultra** (`NG-ASU`) | **12 Months (1 Year)** | **18 Months (1.5 Years)** |

> **How to activate 18-month warranty**: Register the product within 30 days of purchase at `https://novagear.com/register` with your email and order number.

---

## 2. What Is Covered Under Warranty
The warranty covers functional failures arising from normal, intended consumer use:
- **ApexStand Ultra**:
  - Stripped hinge threading from factory assembly.
  - Hinge friction dampers failing to maintain tension under rated load (up to 13.2 lbs).
  - Structural weld or joint fractures.
- **VoltBoost Pro 20K**:
  - Failure to power on, charge, or discharge.
  - OLED display failure, persistent error codes (`E01`, `E02`) without attached loads.
  - Abnormal battery degradation: Premature loss of greater than **40% of original capacity within the first 12 months** under normal operating cycles.
- **NexusCord 100W Cable**:
  - E-Marker chip failure (failing to negotiate fast charging or power delivery).
  - Internal wire severance or loss of electrical continuity without exterior jacket rupture.
  - Connector detachment from cable housing under normal plugging/unplugging force.

---

## 3. What Is NOT Covered (Exclusions)
The warranty does **not** cover:
- Accidental drops, blunt impacts, crushing, or running over by vehicles.
- Water damage, liquid submersion, or corrosion from exposure to corrosive chemicals.
- Damage caused by pet chewing or slicing with sharp objects.
- Unauthorized disassembly, third-party repair modifications, or opening battery enclosures.
- Use of counterfeit wall chargers or electrical surges from non-certified power outlets.
- Normal cosmetic wear and tear (minor anodizing scratches, slight cable fabric pilling, fading).
- Free promotional gifts or giveaways.

## 4. Warranty Claim Procedure (RMA Workflow)

```
[Customer Emails Warranty Claim]
         |
         v
[Support verifies Order # + Video/Photo of defect]
         |
         +--> If issue is simple/cable: [Replacement shipped immediately]
         |
         +--> If complex/battery: [Prepaid label sent to inspect old unit, replacement queued]
```

To file a claim, the customer must email **warranty@novagear.com** with:
1. **Valid Proof of Purchase**: NovaGear Order Number (`NG-XXXXXX`), Amazon Order ID (from authorized NovaGear Storefront), or official retail receipt.
2. **Serial Number**: Located on the product or original retail packaging (e.g., `SN: VB2408-XXXXX`).
3. **Short Description & Demonstration**: A clear 10-second photo or video showing the defect (e.g., demonstrating that the cable does not charge across multiple wall blocks, or showing the power bank error code).

### Resolution:
- For verified defects, NovaGear will ship an identical, brand-new replacement unit at **zero cost to the customer**.
- If the item is out of stock or discontinued, NovaGear will offer an upgraded equivalent model or a full store credit voucher.
- In certain situations, our engineering team may provide a prepaid shipping return label so the defective unit can be returned for failure analysis.
