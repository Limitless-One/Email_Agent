# NovaGear Shipping and Delivery Policy

## 1. Domestic Shipping Speeds & Rates (Contiguous United States)

| Shipping Tier | Carrier & Service | Estimated Delivery Time | Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Shipping** | USPS Ground Advantage / UPS Ground | **3 to 5 business days** | **FREE** on orders $40.00+<br>*(Otherwise $4.99 flat-rate)* |
| **Expedited Shipping** | UPS 2nd Day Air / FedEx 2Day | **2 business days** | **$9.99** flat-rate |
| **Priority Overnight** | UPS Next Day Air / FedEx Priority | **1 business day** (Order by 1:00 PM EST) | **$19.99** flat-rate |

*Note: Delivery times apply to business days (Monday through Friday, excluding Federal holidays).*

---

## 2. Order Fulfillment Timelines
- **Cut-Off Time**: Orders placed before **1:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST)** Monday through Friday are processed and dispatched the **same day**.
- Orders placed after 1:00 PM EST or during weekends/holidays will ship out on the **next business day**.
- Once shipped, a shipping confirmation email containing the **carrier tracking number and live link** is automatically sent to the customer's email address.

---

## 3. Shipping to Alaska, Hawaii, APO/FPO, & US Territories
- Orders shipping to AK, HI, Puerto Rico, Guam, and military APO/FPO addresses are shipped via **USPS Priority Mail**.
- Estimated delivery window: **5 to 9 business days**.
- Standard shipping fee: $7.99 (or free on orders over $60.00).

---

## 4. International Shipping
- We currently ship to **Canada, United Kingdom, European Union, and Australia**.
- **Shipping Times**: 7 to 14 business days via DHL Express / FedEx International Connect.
- **Customs & Duties**: All international orders are billed as **DDU (Delivered Duty Unpaid)** unless specified at checkout. Any local import taxes, VAT, or customs clearance fees assessed by local destination authorities are the responsibility of the recipient.

---

## 5. Tracking Issues & Package Resolution

### Tracking Shows "Delivered" but Package is Missing:
Carrier drivers frequently mark packages as delivered up to 24 hours before physical drop-off, or leave packages in concealed areas.
1. Check with household members, apartment concierge/front desk, and immediate neighbors.
2. Check side doors, porches, garage areas, and bushes.
3. If the package has still not appeared after **24 hours**, contact customer support at **support@novagear.com** with your Order Number.
4. NovaGear will file a formal Carrier Tracer Investigation. If not located within 48 hours, we will dispatch a **free replacement shipment or provide a full refund**.

### Tracking is Stalled / In Transit Exception:
If a tracking status has not updated for more than **5 consecutive business days**, report it to support for lost-in-transit claims processing.
