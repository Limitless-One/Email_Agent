# NovaGear Store - Frequently Asked Questions (General)

### Q1: What payment methods do you accept?
**A:** We accept all major payment methods through our 256-bit SSL encrypted checkout:
- **Credit / Debit Cards**: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, Diners Club, JCB.
- **Digital Wallets**: Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay.
- **Online Gateways**: PayPal, Klarna (Pay in 4 interest-free installments).
*Note: We do not accept personal checks, money orders, cash-on-delivery (COD), or crypto payments.*

### Q2: How can I download an official VAT / Tax Invoice or Receipt for my order?
**A:** Official PDF receipts and invoices with breakdown of state sales tax and shipping are automatically attached to your Order Confirmation Email. If you need a company-addressed VAT/tax invoice with your corporate Tax ID / VAT Number printed:
1. Email **support@novagear.com** with your Order Number and company details (Legal Company Name, Registered Address, Tax ID / EIN / VAT #).
2. Our billing desk will generate and email a customized PDF commercial invoice within 1 business day.

### Q3: Do you offer bulk discounts for corporate, educational, or B2B purchasing?
**A:** Yes! We offer tiered volume pricing for corporate office setups, remote work stipends, schools, and IT departments starting at 10+ units:
- **10 – 49 units**: 15% discount
- **50 – 199 units**: 22% discount
- **200+ units**: 30% discount + custom laser engraving options
- To request a formal quote or submit a corporate Purchase Order (PO), contact **sales@novagear.com** or fill out the form at `https://novagear.com/business`.

### Q4: Are NovaGear products sold by unauthorized third-party sellers covered under warranty?
**A:** NovaGear's official warranty is strictly valid for units purchased directly from:
- `novagear.com` (Official Store)
- `Amazon.com` (Sold directly by "NovaGear Direct" and Fulfilled by Amazon)
- Authorized retail partners (Best Buy, B&H Photo).
Purchases from unauthorized third-party liquidators, eBay resellers, or flea markets are not covered under manufacturer warranty due to the risk of counterfeit or tampered battery cells.

### Q5: How do I apply a discount code or promo voucher to my order?
**A:** At the checkout page, enter your promo code in the "Gift card or discount code" box on the right-hand summary screen and click "Apply". 
- Promo codes must be applied *before* finalizing payment.
- Promo codes cannot be stacked (only one discount code per order).
- If your code failed to apply due to a glitch, email **support@novagear.com** within 24 hours of ordering with your order number and the promo code; our team will manually apply the price difference as a post-purchase refund.

### Q6: How do you protect customer data and payment security?
**A:** NovaGear complies fully with PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance. We never store or view raw credit card numbers on our servers; all payment transactions are tokenized via Stripe and PayPal. Customer personal data is never sold to third-party data brokers. For details, view our Privacy Policy at `https://novagear.com/privacy`.
